hello, i’m almira! ♡

Almira Velasquez

software developer · graphic artist & illustrator

As both an artist and a developer, I’ve always loved finding ways to bring creativity and technology together.

I know how exciting it is to bring an idea to life, but I also know how overwhelming it can be to keep track of commissions, updates, and deadlines, especially when everything is scattered across different messages and platforms.

That’s why I created this little corner of the internet!

I wanted to make something that could help both artists and clients enjoy a smoother, more organized commission experience. A place where artists can focus more on creating, and clients can easily follow along as their ideas come to life.

This website is my way of making the commission process a little less stressful, a little more transparent, and a lot more enjoyable for everyone.

Thank you for being here and giving my little project a chance. I hope it makes your creative journey just a bit easier. ♡

with love,
almira ✿
